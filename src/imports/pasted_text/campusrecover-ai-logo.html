Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear policies on student privacy, claims, and verified property return.

Is my personal identity visible to the person who found my item?

expand_more
Never. All communication is routed through an end-to-end encrypted anonymous relay. Your student email, phone number, and physical dorm address are kept completely confidential. Physical retrieval happens strictly at authorized university desks.

How does the AI verify ownership for items without serial numbers?

expand_more
For items like jewelry, clothing, or backpacks, our Multimodal Vision engine cross-references micro-features (scratches, pin placements, wear patterns, keychain charms) combined with geofence proximity and exact disappearance timestamps. Claimants are also challenged to identify hidden contents before custody transfer.

Can someone falsely claim my expensive laptop or smartphone?

expand_more
No. High-value electronics require either biometric unlocking in person at the desk, matching serial numbers registered to your student hardware portal, or proof of receipt/warranty IMEI verification. False claims trigger immediate academic conduct review via single sign-on logs.

Where can I drop off an item I found on campus?

expand_more
You can drop items off at any of our 18 designated campus nodes, including the Main Student Union Desk (Bldg 420), Green Library Circulation, Tresidder Information Counter, or Campus Police Headquarters. Simply log it first in the app, and staff will generate a fast check-in receipt.

Are finder rewards permitted on the platform?

expand_more
CampusRecover operates primarily on civic duty and campus community honor. However, owners can opt to provide university meal card credits or campus bookstore gift vouchers through our automated university reward escrow upon verified pickup.

Lost something on campus today?

Don't wait. Our neural vision matching searches live items logged across all 18 campus desks simultaneously.
